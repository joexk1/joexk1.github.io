Personal AI · ownership · practical limits

Sovereign AI on your Mac and iPhone.

Fifteen practical notes on where an assistant keeps its memory, what “local” does and does not mean, and how JoeBro works across Mac and iPhone.

Written by Joe, creator of JoeBro. These are product notes, not independent reviews. AI tools assisted with drafting. The drafts were checked against the repository documentation.

Start with the data boundaryJoeBro stores workspace data on the Mac, but prompts go to the model endpoint the user selects. Cloak Mode replaces detected sensitive values; it does not make hosted inference local or guarantee every sensitive detail is removed. The documented iPhone setup pairs to a Mac that must remain open and awake.
